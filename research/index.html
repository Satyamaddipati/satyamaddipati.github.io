Learning to Search From a Teacher Who Already Knows

Cost-aware retrieval learning for LLM agents.

Research question

Can a smaller language model learn when to search and what to retrieve from a teacher that already has access to the evidence, rather than simply imitating the teacher's final answers?

Setup

  • Student: Qwen3-1.7B
  • Teacher: a frozen 4B model with access to supporting evidence
  • Explicit tool-call cost included in the training signal, so the student must trade off answer quality against how much it searches

Evaluation

  • Controlled fact-finding tasks
  • MuSiQue multi-hop QA
  • Metrics: answer accuracy, retrieval cost, and abstention

Baselines

  • Standard knowledge distillation (imitate the teacher's answer, not its search behavior)
  • Information-gain search (retrieve greedily by expected information gain, no learned cost model)

Status

Training and evaluation are underway. Results are not yet available — this entry will be updated as experiments complete.

Also forming a question about KV-cache eviction during long-context reasoning — which parts of an LLM's key-value cache are safe to drop during long, multi-step reasoning without losing answer quality. Too early for a full entry: no method or baselines defined yet.

## Generation prompts

The first four prompts used this exact prefix and suffix around each scene:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial photograph for a personal website. Create [SCENE]. Quiet muted film photography, natural texture and imperfections, warm neutral tones, understated and realistic. Landscape 3:2 composition suitable for crops. No branding, no readable text, no watermark. Output a single photograph, not a website or collage.

Scenes:

- mountains: a mountain hiking trail through alpine meadows and dark evergreen trees toward dramatic rocky peaks with a little snow, pale blue sky, expansive landscape
- notebook: a lived-in wooden desk beside a window, open handwritten notebook and pen in the foreground, plain cream ceramic coffee mug, stacked books and laptop edge behind, soft window light
- coffee: close view of a dark ceramic cappuccino cup with delicate leaf latte art on a saucer on a cafe table, warm natural side light
- gym: a row of black iron dumbbells on a rack beside tall gym windows, soft daylight, no people

Whiteboard prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial website project card photograph. A real whiteboard in a quiet research office, covered with loose hand-drawn boxes, arrows, short indistinct notes for a multi-agent system, with the edge of a wooden desk and a small coffee cup below. Wide 3:2 landscape photograph. Soft natural window light, muted warm grays and cream, slight film grain, candid academic atmosphere. No people, no branding, no readable titles, no watermark. Single photograph only.

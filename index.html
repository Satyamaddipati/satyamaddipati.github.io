About Me

Hi, I’m Satya.

I’m a software and machine learning engineer who builds reliable systems from backend services to production AI.

I’m pursuing an M.S. in Computer Sciences at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. Before graduate school, I worked at Tata AIA Life Insurance, progressing from Management Trainee to Assistant Manager in Data Science.

At Tata AIA, I built FastAPI services, RAG pipelines, LLM evaluation systems, and document-intelligence models. My work moved beyond prototypes: it ran in production, handled large evaluation volumes, and reduced manual work.

I previously studied Environmental Science and Engineering at IIT Bombay. My research there used time-series modeling and machine learning to forecast air pollution and explain the factors driving it.

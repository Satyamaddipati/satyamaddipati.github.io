Fall 2026 research · In progress

Learning to Search from a Teacher Who Already Knows

Can a smaller language model learn when to look for evidence, rather than just imitate a teacher’s answers? With Dheeraj Dhillon, I’m designing a search distillation study that gives the teacher access to evidence while the student must choose what to read.

Language ModelsSearchModel Distillation
Research plan

We plan to compare the student with plain distillation and information-gain search on answer accuracy, retrieval cost, and abstention when evidence is unavailable. The study starts with controlled synthetic tasks, then moves to MuSiQue multi-hop questions. The method and experiments are still being developed; results are forthcoming.

About

A little more about me.

I’m Satya, an M.S. Computer Sciences student at UW–Madison. I build software and applied AI systems.

The path here

I studied Environmental Science and Engineering at IIT Bombay, earning a B.Tech + M.Tech dual degree with Honors. Working with environmental data drew me toward machine learning; research and teaching gave me more questions to follow.

At Tata AIA Life Insurance, I progressed from Management Trainee (July 2023 – June 2024) to Assistant Manager, Data Science (June 2024 – December 2025). I worked on production AI services: retrieval workflows, document classification, and LLM evaluation. I’m now using graduate school to deepen my CS foundations through coursework and projects.

What holds my attention

Machine learning, computer vision, intelligent systems, and distributed systems. My projects range from estimating locations from images to making captured audio easier to revisit.
